Water Damage Restoration in Woodsdale-Edgwood, Wheeling, WV

Historic homes, a creek nearby and a National Road corridor that took on water in June 2025.

☎ (740) 231-9132.

Two names, one historic residential area

Woodsdale and Edgwood are both names Wheelers use for neighborhoods on the east side of the city. The City of Wheeling lists Woodsdale in its 4th Ward and Edgwood in its 5th Ward. The two are also joined in the Woodsdale-Edgwood Neighborhood Historic District, listed on the National Register of Historic Places in 1997, so this page covers them together instead of splitting one area in two.

According to the district listing, it is primarily residential and holds 969 contributing buildings, built between 1888 and 1945. The styles include Queen Anne, Shingle Style, Tudor Revival, American Foursquare, Colonial Revival and Bungalow, along with four Lustron houses. The district is roughly bounded by streets such as Orchard Road, Edgwood Street and Carmel Road, and by Wheeling Creek.

Water damage in houses of this age

A home built between 1888 and 1945 may have been renovated many times, with plumbing, wiring and finishes from several eras. We do not assume any house has a particular pipe material. What we do know is that an older house can hide moisture behind trim, under original flooring and inside walls that were opened and closed more than once.

If your home has original woodwork or floors, tell the crew before work starts. Ask what will be dried in place, what will be removed and why. A written scope with room names helps, and the wall and drywall page explains how wet wall material is judged.

What happened here in June 2025

The News-Sentinel reported that Long Run, a tributary of Wheeling Creek, overflowed on the Saturday night of June 14, 2025 and flooded National Road through Woodsdale. Homes, apartments, basements and garages took on water, and Overbrook Avenue and Edgewood Street were named in the coverage. Residents spent the following days removing mud, debris and ruined belongings.

That is one documented event. It does not show that every house in Woodsdale or Edgwood floods. Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status not verified. Look up your own address on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center before drawing any conclusion.

If water reaches a basement or garage

  • Stay out of standing water that could reach outlets, cords or a panel..
  • Do not carry electrical equipment into a wet space..
  • Shut off the water supply only if the valve is dry and easy to reach..
  • Photograph the water line and the damaged items from a safe spot..
  • Move stored belongings that are still dry, if you can do so without entering the water..

Clean water from a supply line can often be removed and dried with extraction, air movers and dehumidifiers. Creek or storm water is treated as contaminated, which changes what can be saved. The basement water damage page walks through both cases.

Drying a house with a lot of woodwork

Drying is measured, not guessed. A crew takes moisture readings in floors, walls and framing, runs air movers and dehumidifiers, and rechecks until the readings come down. Wood can cup or swell if it dries too fast or stays wet too long, so the pace matters. The hardwood floor page covers what to expect.

Documenting the loss

Keep a dated record. Photos of the source and the damage, the receipts for emergency costs and the crew's moisture readings all help when you talk to your insurer. Whether a loss is covered depends on your policy and the cause, and we do not promise coverage or a claim result.

Local note for Woodsdale-Edgwood

Facts here come from the City of Wheeling's ward list, the National Register listing for the Woodsdale-Edgwood district and the June 2025 News-Sentinel report on Woodsdale. The city says that demolishing a contributing structure in a city historic district needs review by its Historic Landmarks Commission, so ask the city before planning removal of structural parts.

Nearby Wheeling Creek also runs through Elm Grove. Return to the service area page for other neighborhoods.

Questions from Woodsdale-Edgwood residents

Why is this page called Woodsdale-Edgwood?

The two names are joined in one National Register historic district, so we cover them on one page instead of repeating the same area.

Did Woodsdale flood in June 2025?

The News-Sentinel reported that Long Run overflowed and flooded National Road through Woodsdale on June 14, 2025.

Do I need the city's approval for repairs in a historic district?

Ask the city. Its Historic Landmarks Commission reviews demolition of contributing structures, and plumbing, electrical or structural work may also need permits.

Can old wood floors be saved after a leak?

Often, if the water was clean and drying starts quickly. Readings decide, not appearance.
