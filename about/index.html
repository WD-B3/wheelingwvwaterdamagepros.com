About Wheeling WV Water Damage Pros

A 24/7 dispatch line for water damage in Wheeling and the upper Ohio Valley.

☎ (740) 231-9132.

Who we are

Wheeling WV Water Damage Pros is a water damage restoration service for homes and businesses in Wheeling, West Virginia and the nearby river towns. Our office is at 590 National Rd, Wheeling, WV 26003. We are licensed and insured.

We built this service around a simple idea. When water is coming through a ceiling at midnight, you need someone to answer the phone, explain what to do and send help. The dispatch line is open around the clock for that reason.

How we work

  • We answer questions first and explain options before work begins..
  • We explain scope and price before work starts, and tell you when the plan changes..
  • We take moisture readings and photos so there is a record of the loss and the drying..
  • We use the ANSI/IICRC S500 water categories and classes as a planning reference..
  • We do not promise what an insurer will pay, and we do not promise exact arrival times..

Our water extraction and structural drying pages show how the work is done in more detail.

Where we work

We serve Wheeling, Triadelphia, Benwood, Glen Dale, Moundsville, Wellsburg and nearby communities along the Ohio River. These towns share older housing, river flood history and steep terrain, and those conditions shape how we plan a job.

What you can expect from a call

The dispatcher asks about the source of the water, how much there is, which rooms are affected and whether anyone is at risk. Safety comes first. You will get steps you can take right away, such as shutting off water or moving valuables, and an explanation of what a crew would do next.

What we do not do

We do not promise insurance outcomes, and we do not promise exact arrival times. We do not use scare tactics to push work. If a problem is small enough to handle yourself, we will say so. If a job needs a plumber, an electrician or a building inspector, we will tell you.

We also keep our service descriptions plain. Each service page lists the steps in order, the tools used and the signs that mean you should call.

The dispatch process

  1. A call comes in to the 24/7 line..
  2. The dispatcher records the address, the water source and the rooms affected..
  3. Safety instructions are given, including how to shut off water and avoid electrical hazards..
  4. A crew is assigned and the dispatcher shares what to expect..
  5. The scope and price are explained before work begins..
  6. Moisture readings and photos are recorded during the job..

This process is simple on purpose. When the situation is stressful, clear steps help. The services page lists every type of loss we handle, and the service area page shows where we work.

Standards we reference

We use the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard as a reference for water damage. It defines categories of water by contamination level and classes of loss by how much water was absorbed. Those two ideas shape how a job is planned, from the protective gear to the equipment count. They are explained in plain language on our water extraction and sewage backup pages.

Our approach to scope and price

Every job starts with a conversation about what is wet, what caused it and what you want to protect. From there, the crew prepares a scope in plain language. It lists the rooms, the steps and the equipment, and the price is explained before work begins. If the plan changes, we update it in writing.

This approach takes a little more time up front, but it saves confusion later. It also gives you a document you can share with your insurer or compare with another quote.

We believe you should understand what you are paying for. If a line does not make sense, ask. A good answer should take a minute, not a sales pitch.

Working with insurers and property managers

Many of our calls involve an insurance claim, a landlord or a property manager. We keep photos, moisture readings and dated notes, and we explain them in simple terms. We do not decide coverage, and we do not tell you what a claim will pay. We make sure the facts about the loss are written down clearly, so the people who decide have what they need.

For commercial buildings and rentals, we coordinate access, keys and work hours with the person in charge. The commercial water damage page explains how those jobs are planned.

Contact and next steps

If you have a question about how a job works, call and ask. If you are comparing companies, ask for a written scope and compare line by line. If you have water in your home right now, call and let the dispatcher guide you. You can find more detail on the contact page.
