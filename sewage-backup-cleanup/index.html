Emergency Sewage Backup Cleanup in Wheeling, WV

Sewage backups involve contaminated water and real health hazards. Know what to do first and why cleanup is not a mop job.

Sewage backup cleanup in Wheeling, WV

Sewage backup cleanup is the safe removal, disinfection and drying of a building after wastewater comes up through a floor drain, toilet, tub or sewer cleanout. In Wheeling, WV, a backup can come from a blockage in your own lateral line or from the public sewer. Either way, the water is treated as contaminated.

This is different from a clean water leak. Do not treat sewage like a spill. The safest course is to keep people out of the area and call for help.

Sewage backup: what to do first

A sewage backup is an emergency. The dispatch line is open 24/7, including nights and weekends, and same-day response is offered when available.

  1. Keep everyone, especially children and pets, out of the affected area..
  2. Stop using water. Do not flush, run the dishwasher or run the washing machine..
  3. Do not walk in the water or touch electrical equipment while standing in it. Call the utility or 911 about electrical or gas risks before you call a restoration line..
  4. Open windows if it is safe, but do not run household fans or a vacuum in the contaminated area..
  5. Photograph the area from a safe place and call the dispatch line..

Why does the drain gurgle and smell like sewage?

People often notice trouble before the water appears. Gurgling drains, toilets that bubble when another fixture runs, slow drains in several fixtures at once and a rotten egg smell near a floor drain all point to a blockage in the main line. Once water comes up, the situation has moved from a plumbing problem to a contamination problem.

Sewage backup cleanup is the name of the trade service, and Category 3 water is the technical term for water carrying sewage or similar contaminants. Both terms describe the same job.

How cleanup is done safely

Crews wear protective gear and set up containment if needed. Water is extracted with pumps and vacuums, then porous materials that cannot be cleaned are removed. Hard surfaces are cleaned and disinfected, and the area is dried. Industry practice for contaminated water is covered in the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard, and you can ask any company whether its crews follow it.

Public sewer or private line

Responsibility is different depending on where the blockage is. Wheeling has a Water Pollution Control department as part of its public works, and the city handles the public sewer system. A blockage in the line from your house to the city main may be the property owner's responsibility, but this varies. Call the city and ask where public responsibility ends before you pay for a repair.

A plumber can run a camera down the line to find the cause. Tree roots, grease, broken pipe and collapsed sections are common. If the backup came from the public main, tell the city and document the date and time.

Health and safety

Sewage can carry bacteria and other pathogens. Avoid contact with skin, do not eat food that was exposed and do not let children play in the area. Health questions belong to a physician. If someone feels unwell after exposure, call a doctor.

Do not use bleach and ammonia products together, and do not mix cleaners. Professional cleanup uses products and methods intended for the job.

Cost factors and insurance

Price depends on the volume of water, how long it sat, how many rooms and floors were affected, the materials that need to be removed and how many drying days are needed. Insurance coverage for sewer backup is often a separate endorsement on a homeowners policy. Check your policy or ask your insurer whether you have sewer or drain backup coverage. Questions about Sewage Backup Cleanup in Wheeling

Do you handle sewage backup emergencies at night or on weekends in Wheeling?

Yes. Sewage can appear at any hour and the dispatch line is open 24/7. Keep out of the area and call. A crew is sent as soon as one is available. Same-day response is offered when available.

Can I clean up sewage myself?

It is not recommended. Sewage carries contaminants and porous materials usually have to be removed. Professional crews use protective gear and methods meant for it.

Who is responsible for a sewer backup in Wheeling?

It depends on where the blockage is. Contact the city to ask where public responsibility ends and use a plumber to find the cause.

Does homeowners insurance cover sewer backup?

Often only with a specific endorsement. Check your policy or ask your insurer.

Is a floor drain backup the same thing?

Yes, in practice. Water coming up through a floor drain is treated the same as other sewage backups.
