Triadelphia sits on the I-70 and US 40 corridor east of Wheeling

Triadelphia is a small town in Ohio County, about six miles from Wheeling by road. The Wheeling-area write-ups describe it as a suburb served by I-70 and US 40, the old National Road, and its population is around 650. For water damage restoration in Triadelphia, WV 26059, that means a short drive from the Wheeling dispatch point on the main highway rather than a long trip on a back road.

The corridor matters in practice. A crew heading out on a night call has a direct, well traveled route. The town sits in the same county as Wheeling, so county level resources such as emergency management and the Ohio County health and planning offices are the same ones you would use in the city.

When water is spreading in Triadelphia

If water is moving across a floor, down a wall or out of a ceiling right now, treat it as an emergency and call the 24/7 line. Emergency water removal is available around the clock for Triadelphia, and a crew goes out as soon as one is free. Same-day response is offered when available.

Use the minutes before a crew reaches you. The cards below are written for a home in this corridor, where a main water valve is often in a basement or utility room and the electrical panel may be in the same space.

Town water and sewer work is going on right now

The Town of Triadelphia provides its own water and sewer service, and the town reports a Water and Sewer Project that began in June 2024. Crews are replacing water lines, adding new meters and cleaning and repairing sewer lines. Infrastructure work like this can change water pressure and stir up sediment in lines, and any construction near a service line is a reason to know where your main valve is.

If a leak looks like it comes from the town side of the meter, call the town. The town lists a contact number and a contact page on its website. For anything inside the house, call our line and a plumber separately. Water and sewer questions about your account belong with the town, not with us.

What Triadelphia homes tend to call about

The town sits near hills and the I-70 and US 40 roads, and many of its houses have basements. The calls that come in are mostly interior failures, such as a supply line that lets go, a water heater that gives out or a drain that backs up. River flooding is a smaller part of the picture here than on the Ohio River towns to the west, but check the FEMA flood map for your address instead of assuming either way.

Do you cover every ZIP code in Triadelphia?

Is emergency water removal available in Triadelphia?

Yes. The dispatch line is answered 24/7, including nights and weekends. A crew is sent as soon as one is available, and same-day response is offered when available.

Who do I call if the leak is on the town side of the meter?

The Town of Triadelphia, which provides water and sewer service. If the problem is inside your house, call a plumber and call us for the water cleanup.

Does the ongoing town water project affect my pipes?
