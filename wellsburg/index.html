Wellsburg has had real river flood emergencies in recent years

Wellsburg is the Brooke County seat on the Ohio River, about 17 miles north of Wheeling on WV 2. Its population is around 2,300. In April 2024 the city was placed under a state of emergency because of flooding, according to an announcement from the Brooke County Emergency Management Agency, and streets in the southern end of the city were reported under water. That event is the reason water damage restoration in Wellsburg, WV 26070, starts with flood readiness.

The same week, the river at Wheeling reached about 41.85 feet according to local reports, which shows how the whole valley rises and falls together.

Reading the river gauge

The National Weather Service tracks the Ohio River at Wellsburg. The gauge lists the action stage at 28.2 feet, minor flooding at 36 feet, moderate at 39 feet and major at 41 feet. The record crest listed is 54.6 feet on March 19, 1936. Those numbers give a reference, so when a forecast says the river will reach a given stage you can match it to what happened before.

A homeowner can use the gauge in a simple way. Look at the forecast stage, then check the FEMA flood map for your address to see whether your lot is in a mapped flood area. Neither tool replaces your own judgment, and neither tells you whether your policy covers the damage.

Insurance questions after river water

Flood damage is usually excluded from a standard homeowners policy and needs separate flood coverage. The West Virginia Offices of the Insurance Commissioner points people to the National Flood Insurance Program for flood insurance. The state office also lists a consumer services line for policy questions. Read your policy and ask your insurer or agent before an event, not after it.
