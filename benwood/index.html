The usual problem in Benwood is water coming up from the river side

Benwood is a river town in Marshall County, about six miles southwest of Wheeling on WV 2. Wheeling area descriptions treat it as part of the Wheeling metropolitan area, and the city article notes that parts of the Wheeling area, including Benwood, Mozart and Sherrard, extend into Marshall County. Water damage restoration in Benwood, WV 26031, often starts with a question about how close the property is to the river.

Population is around 1,200, and the town is compact, which means a basement or low first floor is usually only a few blocks from the water. That proximity is the starting point for deciding whether a problem is a plumbing failure or a river event.

When water is spreading in Benwood

Water that is spreading through a Benwood home is an emergency at any hour. Emergency water removal is available here around the clock. Call the 24/7 line and a crew is sent as soon as one is available. Same-day response is offered when available.

Because river and plumbing losses call for different safety steps, tell the dispatcher whether the water came from inside the house or from outside. River or surface water is treated as contaminated.

Who to call for a leak, and who to call for cleanup

Benwood runs its own Water Department and Sewer Department. The city asks residents to report water leaks during business hours, which it lists as 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. After hours, the city directs callers to its Police Department. If you see water coming from a street main or from the meter, report it to the city.

Our dispatch line is separate. It handles the cleanup and drying inside your property. It does not replace calling the city for a public main problem, and it does not replace a plumber for a repair inside the house.

River height and the lower parts of town

The National Weather Service tracks the Ohio River with gauges along the valley. At the Wellsburg gauge upriver, the flood categories are listed as minor at 36 feet, moderate at 39 feet and major at 41 feet, and the record is 54.6 feet on March 19, 1936. Benwood has its own reading in the same river system, and the relevant gauge for your street is something to check on the NOAA water site instead of guessing.

The practical reading is that river towns like Benwood feel river rises first in their lowest rooms. If you have a basement, know where the sump pump and the main valve are, and check the FEMA flood map for your address before a high water forecast arrives.

Do you cover every ZIP code in Benwood?

Is emergency water removal available in Benwood?

Who handles water leaks on the street in Benwood?

The City of Benwood Water Department, during its business hours, and the police department after hours. Our dispatch line is for cleanup inside your property.

Does river flooding in Benwood count as a plumbing leak?

No. River water is treated as contaminated, and flood damage is usually excluded from standard homeowners policies. Check your policy or ask your insurer.
