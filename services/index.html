Nineteen ways we help after water gets in

Every water loss is different, so each service has its own page with the steps, the warning signs and answers to common questions. Pick the one that matches your situation, or call and a dispatcher will help you choose. All services are available through the same 24/7 line.

Pipes and plumbing

Burst Pipe Water Damage

Water removal and drying after a supply line splits. See burst pipe water damage.

Frozen Pipe Water Damage

Thaw related leaks and the damage they leave in walls and ceilings. See frozen pipe water damage.

Water Heater Leak Cleanup

Drying and cleanup after a tank leak or a failed fitting. See water heater leak cleanup.

Slab Leak Water Damage

Moisture from leaks in lines under or inside a concrete slab. See slab leak water damage.

Appliances and HVAC

Appliance Leak Water Damage

Washers, dishwashers, refrigerators and other appliance leaks. See appliance leak water damage.

AC Leak Water Damage

Condensate line and drain pan overflows from air conditioners. See AC leak water damage.

Kitchen Water Damage

Cabinets, flooring and walls wet from sinks or appliances. See kitchen water damage.

Weather and building envelope

Ceiling Water Damage Repair

Wet ceilings, sagging drywall and leaks from the floor above. See ceiling water damage repair.

Storm Water Damage

Roof leaks, wind driven rain and runoff after severe weather. See storm water damage.

Flood Damage Cleanup

Cleanup after river, creek or storm flooding reaches the building. See flood damage cleanup.

Basement Water Damage

Water removal and drying for finished and unfinished basements. See basement water damage.

Crawl Space Water Damage

Standing water and damp framing below the first floor. See crawl space water damage.

Water removal and drying

Water Extraction

Pumps and extractors remove standing water from floors and carpet. See water extraction.

Structural Drying

Air movers and dehumidifiers that dry framing and subfloors. See structural drying.

Sewage Backup Cleanup

Contaminated water cleanup using protective methods. See sewage backup cleanup.

Floors and walls

Hardwood Floor Water Damage

Drying and assessing cupped or lifting wood floors. See hardwood floor water damage.

Carpet Water Damage

Carpet and pad drying, cleaning or replacement. See carpet water damage.

Wall and Drywall Water Damage

Wet drywall, insulation and baseboards in wall cavities. See wall and drywall water damage.

Commercial

Commercial Water Damage

Water loss response for shops, offices and other buildings. See commercial water damage.

Not sure which service you need?

Water damage often involves more than one service. A burst pipe can lead to extraction, structural drying and drywall repair in the same job. Call the 24/7 line, describe what you see, and the dispatcher will explain which steps apply.

Questions about our services

Which service do I need first?

Usually water extraction, followed by structural drying. The dispatcher can confirm the order for your situation.

Can one job include several services?

Yes. Many losses combine extraction, drying and repairs.

Are all services available at night?

The dispatch line is open 24/7. Crew availability depends on workload and conditions.
