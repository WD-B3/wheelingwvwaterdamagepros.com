Carpet water damage restoration in Wheeling, WV

Carpet water damage restoration is the extraction, drying and cleaning of carpet and carpet pad after water soaks it. In Wheeling, WV, wet carpet shows up after a burst pipe, a washing machine overflow, a basement seep or a flood. The carpet is the part you see, but the pad and the subfloor below it are where water stays.

The decision to save or replace carpet depends on the water category and how long it was wet.

When wet carpet is urgent

Wet carpet is urgent because water spreads outward and downward, and mold can start in damp carpet and pad within a short time. Call the 24/7 dispatch line if carpet is soaked. Same-day response is offered when available. Do not wait for it to dry on its own.

  • Remove furniture and put aluminum foil or blocks under legs..
  • Lift small rugs and hang them to dry..
  • Do not use a household vacuum on wet carpet..
  • Do not walk on wet carpet near outlets..
  • Open windows if weather allows and run fans only if outlets are dry..

Carpet or pad, what stays and what goes

The pad is the least expensive part and the hardest to dry, which is why it is often replaced even when the carpet is kept. Water category is explained on the flood page and the sewage page.

Drying carpet in place

In a clean water loss, crews extract water with a wand, lift the carpet at one edge and float it so air can get underneath, then run air movers and a dehumidifier. The pad may be removed and replaced while the carpet dries. Meters check the subfloor.

Carpet that was tacked down and can be re-stretched is often saved. Carpet that has delaminated, has stains from the backing or has an odor is a candidate for replacement.

Odors and what they mean

A musty or sour smell from carpet means it stayed damp too long. Cleaning the surface does not cure it, because the odor source is in the pad and subfloor. Drying fast and removing the pad is the main prevention.

Carpet in basements

Carpet over concrete in a basement can look fine while the concrete under it stays wet. Check for dampness at the edges and for a smell. If water comes in repeatedly, see the basement page for the below-grade picture.

Cost factors and insurance

Costs vary with the square footage, whether the pad is replaced, the water category and the drying time. Insurance often covers sudden water damage to carpet and excludes gradual leaks. Read your policy or ask your insurer. Want your real number? Call (740) 231-9132.

Related help

Carpet damage often follows an appliance leak or a water heater leak. A wood floor in the same room is covered on the hardwood page.

Testing carpet and pad for moisture

Carpet can feel dry on top while the pad underneath still holds water. A moisture meter pressed through the carpet tells you more than a hand does. Technicians check several spots along walls, at seams and near the original wet area, and record the readings so they can compare them over the next days.

If the pad is wet, it is usually lifted and replaced, since pad is inexpensive and holds contaminants. The carpet itself can often be cleaned and dried in place or lifted, dried on racks and reinstalled, as long as the water was clean and treated quickly.

When replacement is the safer call

  • Water that was gray or contaminated and soaked the backing..
  • Carpet that has been wet for more than a day or two..
  • Visible mold growth or a persistent musty smell after drying..
  • Delaminated backing that separates from the carpet face..

Ask your crew to explain why they recommend saving or replacing the carpet, and to put that reasoning in the scope. It helps with insurance questions and gives you a clear record.

The ANSI/IICRC S500 standard guides whether carpet and pad can be saved based on the water category and how long they were wet.

Questions about Carpet Water Damage in Wheeling

How long can carpet stay wet before mold starts?

It depends on conditions, but damp carpet and pad can support growth within days. Speed matters.

Should I replace the pad?

Often yes. Pad holds water and is cheap compared to the carpet.

Can I dry carpet with fans only?

Fans alone often leave the pad and subfloor wet. A dehumidifier and a moisture check are better.

Can contaminated carpet be cleaned?

Usually it is removed. Carpet exposed to sewage or flood water is normally discarded.

Does insurance cover wet carpet?

Sudden water damage is often covered and flooding is usually excluded. Check your policy or ask your insurer.
